# Online Scam: AI for Catching Online Scams Before Residents Pay

Client: City of San José, Downtown
Team: Desiree Lopez, Aug-Oct 2026
My part:I built the scam-checker prototype and wrote the problem statement. 

## The problem
Downtown residents, including students, renters, and small business owners, regularly receive suspicious texts, listings, and payment requests, and have no quick, trusted way to check them. By the time they find out something was a scam, the money is often gone

## What we built
- What it does:A web prototype that analyzes a suspicious message, image, or screenshot and returns a scam-detection score with the red flags behind it.
- What the user sees: An upload box for a photo, screenshot, or pasted text, then a 0-100 / low-medium-high score and a plain-language explanation of why.
- What a human still decides: The resident decides whether to pay, reply, or report. 

## What I would do next
The score is only as good as its testing, so I would check it against a set of real scam and legitimate messages and add a "what to do next" step linking to local reporting resources.
